Our products

Lisa - your parking companion in Stockholm

Lisa knows which parking rules apply where your car is parked and reminds you in time, for example the evening before street cleaning. The rules come from the City of Stockholm's open data. Neighbours can also help each other with tips when a car is about to be parked illegally. App for iPhone and Android, launching in 2026.
